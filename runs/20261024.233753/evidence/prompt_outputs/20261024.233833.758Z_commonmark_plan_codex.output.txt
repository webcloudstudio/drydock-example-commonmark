## full_test.sh suite-binding choice
The supplied scoring harness checks both the runner exit status and its tally because the runner may exit zero after a modulo-256 failure count. The terminal acceptance therefore relies on the supplied harness's exit code as the conventional narrow response contract for complete-suite success.
