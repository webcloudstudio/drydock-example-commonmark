in pre tags):
</requirement>

Whitespace surrounding block-level tags is removed.
</requirement>
<requirement name="normalize-self-closing-tags" stories="render-normalization">
Self-closing tags are converted to open tags.
</requirement>
<requirement name="normalize-attributes" stories="render-normalization">
Attributes are sorted and lowercased.
</requirement>
<requirement name="normalize-references" stories="inline-escapes,render-normalization">
References are converted to unicode, except that '<', '>', '&', and
      '"' are rendered using entities.
</requirement>
