<requirement name="collapse-inner-whitespace" stories="verification">
Multiple inner whitespaces are collapsed to a single space (except
    in pre tags):
</requirement>
<requirement name="remove-block-tag-whitespace" stories="verification">
* Whitespace surrounding block-level tags is removed.
</requirement>
<requirement name="normalize-self-closing-tags" stories="verification">
* Self-closing tags are converted to open tags.
</requirement>
<requirement name="normalize-attributes" stories="verification">
* Attributes are sorted and lowercased.
</requirement>
<requirement name="normalize-character-references" stories="verification">
* References are converted to unicode, except that '<', '>', '&', and
      '"' are rendered using entities.
</requirement>
<requirement name="preserve-cdata" stories="verification">
# We work around HTMLParser's limitations parsing CDATA
        # by breaking the input into chunks and passing CDATA chunks
        # through verbatim.
</requirement>
